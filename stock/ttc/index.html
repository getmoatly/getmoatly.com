What the numbers say

On the four Ms, Toro Co earns 100 for management and 59 for moat. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 16.1% and a gross margin of 33.4% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. Debt sits at 0.72x equity. A moderate moat alongside exceptional management is a combination worth understanding before the price matters at all.

The valuation runs on assumptions chosen to understate rather than flatter. Growth is modelled at 5.9% a year, the rate measured from the filings. The exit multiple assumed is 11.9x. That produces an intrinsic value of $19.60, a 10-CAP price of $76.87 and a payback time price of $80.45, with the value zone set at the highest of the three, $80.45. Today's price of $97.79 sits 22% above it.

A wonderful business at the wrong price is still the wrong price. Toro Co scores well on the business and poorly on the entry point, which is the most common shape in a long bull market.
