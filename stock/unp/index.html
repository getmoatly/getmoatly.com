What the numbers say

Union Pacific Corporation is scored 39 for moat and 78 for management. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 11.7% and a gross margin of 59.4% — figures that describe a business earning a respectable return on what it employs. Debt sits at 1.72x equity. A narrow moat alongside strong management is a combination worth understanding before the price matters at all.

The price anchors are built on deliberately conservative assumptions.
