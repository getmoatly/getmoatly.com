What the numbers say

On the four Ms, W. W. Grainger earns 100 for management and 73 for moat. A solid moat alongside exceptional management is a combination worth understanding before the price matters at all.

The valuation runs on assumptions chosen to understate rather than flatter. That produces an intrinsic value of $392.03, a 10-CAP price of $361.42 and a payback time price of $429.51, with the value zone set at the highest of the three, $429.51. Today's price of $1,281.68 is 198% above it.

A wonderful business at the wrong price is still the wrong price. W. W. Grainger scores well on the business and poorly on the entry point, which is the most common shape in a long bull market.
