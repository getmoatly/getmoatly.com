Moody's Corporation is scored 86 for moat and 100 for management. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 21.0% and a gross margin of 68.2% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. Debt sits at 1.81x equity.

Three independent anchors set the price worth paying. Today's price of $447.31 is 114% above it.
