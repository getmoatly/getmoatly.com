What the numbers say

Eaton Corporation scores 93 for management and 76 for moat. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 13.1% and a gross margin of 37.6% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. Debt sits at 0.57x equity. A solid moat alongside exceptional management is a combination worth understanding before the price matters at all.

Today's price of $437.28 is 206% above it.
