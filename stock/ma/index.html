Mastercard's moat score of 96 and management score of 100 point to a business with unusually durable advantages and highly efficient use of capital. Return on invested capital of 48.9% and a gross margin of 77.9% support that reading, and even with debt to equity at 2.5x, the combination suggests capital is being deployed effectively rather than propping up weak returns.
