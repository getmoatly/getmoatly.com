What the numbers say

Jabil is scored 81 for moat and 92 for management. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 19.8% and a gross margin of 9.2% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. The balance sheet carries debt at 2.49x equity, which magnifies both the returns above and the risk beneath them.

The price anchors are built on deliberately conservative assumptions. Growth is modelled at 4.2% a year, the rate measured from the filings. The exit multiple assumed is 8.4x. That produces an intrinsic value of $30.43, a 10-CAP price of $169.01 and a payback time price of $163.35, with the value zone set at the highest of the three, $169.01. Today's price of $299.82 is 77% above it.

The operating figures describe a business performing at a high level; the price asks for that performance to continue and then some. A margin of safety score of 5 is the measure of how little room that leaves for being wrong.
