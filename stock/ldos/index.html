What the numbers say

Leidos is scored 86 for moat and 88 for management. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 15.0% and a gross margin of 17.7% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. Debt sits at 1.21x equity.

The price anchors are built on deliberately conservative assumptions. Growth is modelled at 6.9% a year, the rate measured from the filings. The exit multiple assumed is 13.8x. That produces an intrinsic value of $71.42, a 10-CAP price of $173.55 and a payback time price of $189.74, with the value zone set at the highest of the three, $189.74. Today's price of $121.97 sits inside that zone.

A business scoring this well, trading inside its value zone, is the combination the framework exists to find — which is also the reason to check why the market disagrees. A margin of safety score of 29 describes the cushion, not the certainty.
