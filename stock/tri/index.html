What the numbers say

Thomson Reuters Corporation is scored 61 for moat and 93 for management. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 10.1% and a gross margin of 75.8% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. Debt sits at 0.18x equity. A solid moat alongside exceptional management is a combination worth understanding before the price matters at all.

The valuation runs on assumptions chosen to understate rather than flatter. Growth is modelled at 4.6% a year, below the 5.0% actually measured over the last decade — the model caps growth rather than extrapolating a good run forwards. The exit multiple assumed is 9.1x. That produces an intrinsic value of $13.14, a 10-CAP price of $54.96 and a payback time price of $54.00, with the value zone set at the highest of the three, $54.96. Today's price of $99.36 is 81% above it.

A wonderful business at the wrong price is still the wrong price. Thomson Reuters Corporation scores well on the business and poorly on the entry point, which is the most common shape in a long bull market.
