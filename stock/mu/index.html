The price anchors are built on deliberately conservative assumptions. Growth is modelled at 15.0% a year, below the 37.0% actually measured over the last decade — the model caps growth rather than extrapolating a good run forwards. The exit multiple assumed is 30.0x. Today's price of $725.11 sits inside that zone.
