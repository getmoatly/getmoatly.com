What the numbers say

On the four Ms, Ralph Lauren Corporation earns 95 for management and 69 for moat. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 19.6% and a gross margin of 69.9% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. Debt sits at 1.05x equity. The solid moat and exceptional management are not the same judgement, and the gap between them is where the argument about this business sits.

The valuation runs on assumptions chosen to understate rather than flatter. Growth is modelled at 13.0% a year, the rate measured from the filings. The exit multiple assumed is 21.3x. That produces an intrinsic value of $283.36, a 10-CAP price of $183.60 and a payback time price of $264.88, with the value zone set at the highest of the three, $264.88. Today's price of $362.96 is 37% above it.

The operating figures describe a business performing at a high level; the price asks for that performance to continue and then some. A margin of safety score of 39 is the measure of how little room that leaves for being wrong.
