What the numbers say

On the four Ms, Salesforce Inc. earns 73 for management and 89 for moat. Debt sits at 0.29x equity. The wide moat and strong management are not the same judgement, and the gap between them is where the argument about this business sits.

Today's price of $236.69 sits inside that zone.
