What the numbers say

Ametek is scored 80 for moat and 94 for management. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 11.0% and a gross margin of 36.4% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. Debt sits at 0.22x equity.

The price anchors are built on deliberately conservative assumptions. Growth is modelled at 10.3% a year, the rate measured from the filings. The exit multiple assumed is 20.5x. That produces an intrinsic value of $92.27, a 10-CAP price of $81.17 and a payback time price of $103.35, with the value zone set at the highest of the three, $103.35. Today's price of $249.09 is 141% above it.

A wonderful business at the wrong price is still the wrong price. Ametek scores well on the business and poorly on the entry point, which is the most common shape in a long bull market.
