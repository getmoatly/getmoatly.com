What the numbers say

Shopify scores 63 for management and 79 for moat. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 8.7% and a gross margin of 48.1% — figures that describe a business earning a respectable return on what it employs. Debt sits at 0.01x equity.

Three independent anchors set the price worth paying. Growth is modelled at 15.0% a year, below the 32.0% actually measured over the last decade — the model caps growth rather than extrapolating a good run forwards. The exit multiple assumed is 30.0x. That produces an intrinsic value of $44.70, a 10-CAP price of $18.18 and a payback time price of $28.70, with the value zone set at the highest of the three, $28.70. Today's price of $149.09 is 419% above it.

The operating figures describe a business performing at a high level; the price asks for that performance to continue and then some. A margin of safety score of 15 is the measure of how little room that leaves for being wrong.
