What the numbers say

Intuitive Surgical scores 96 for management and 99 for moat. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 13.7% and a gross margin of 66.0% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. Debt sits at 0.02x equity.

Three independent anchors set the price worth paying. Today's price of $401.24 is 171% above it.
