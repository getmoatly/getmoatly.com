MoatlyAI Insights

The moat and management scores of 65 and 60 point to a business with a durable but not dominant position, and capital returns that are respectable rather than exceptional. Return on invested capital sits at 4.2%, gross margin at 59.9%, and debt to equity at 2.6x, a combination consistent with a large, leveraged financial institution rather than a high-return compounder. Free cash flow yield of 11.2% suggests the cash generation is solid relative to the current price.

The valuation model assumes 15% growth, deliberately capped below the 17% the company has actually delivered, and applies a 16x exit multiple. That produces a margin of safety price of $186.13, a 10-cap price of $308.25, and a payback time price of $486.59, which also stands as the consensus buy price. At $362.04, the stock trades above the two more conservative measures but below the payback and consensus figures.

The margin of safety score of 51 reflects that split: the price is asking for confidence that the moderate moat and management figures continue to hold, even though the model already understates the growth history to be cautious.
