What the numbers say

On the four Ms, Cummins earns 88 for management and 77 for moat. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 11.4% and a gross margin of 25.3% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. Debt sits at 0.66x equity. A solid moat alongside exceptional management is a combination worth understanding before the price matters at all.

Three independent anchors set the price worth paying. Growth is modelled at 11.2% a year, the rate measured from the filings. The exit multiple assumed is 22.4x. Today's price of $517.00 is 44% above it.
