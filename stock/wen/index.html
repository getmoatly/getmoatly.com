What the numbers say

Wendy's Co/The is scored 38 for moat and 37 for management. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 5.1% and a gross margin of 26.9% — figures that describe capital earning less than it costs to employ. The balance sheet carries debt at 35.31x equity, which magnifies both the returns above and the risk beneath them.

The price anchors are built on deliberately conservative assumptions. Today's price of $6.25 sits inside that zone.
