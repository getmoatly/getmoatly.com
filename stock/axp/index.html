American Express is scored 86 for moat and 80 for management. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 8.3% and a gross margin of 83.2% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. Debt sits at 1.73x equity.

The price anchors are built on deliberately conservative assumptions. Today's price of $302.09 sits inside that zone.
