What the numbers say

Duke Energy Corporation scores 23 for management and 41 for moat. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 4.1% and a gross margin of 31.6% — figures that describe capital earning less than it costs to employ. Debt sits at 1.75x equity. The moderate moat and weak management are not the same judgement, and the gap between them is where the argument about this business sits.

The valuation runs on assumptions chosen to understate rather than flatter. Growth is modelled at 6.6% a year, below the 7.0% actually measured over the last decade — the model caps growth rather than extrapolating a good run forwards. The exit multiple assumed is 13.3x. That produces an intrinsic value of $41.67, a 10-CAP price of $58.46 and a payback time price of $63.16, with the value zone set at the highest of the three, $63.16. Today's price of $113.74 is 80% above it.
