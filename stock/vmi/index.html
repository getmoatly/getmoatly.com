What the numbers say

On the four Ms, Valmont Industries earns 96 for management and 54 for moat. The management score rests on a return on invested capital of 18.5% and a gross margin of 30.2% — figures that describe a business turning capital into profit efficiently. Debt sits at 0.57x equity. The moderate moat and exceptional management are not the same judgement, and the gap between them is where the argument about this business sits.

The price anchors are built on deliberately conservative assumptions. Growth is modelled at 7.2% a year, the rate measured from the filings. The exit multiple assumed is 14.4x. That produces an intrinsic value of $180.47, a 10-CAP price of $188.56 and a payback time price of $209.14, with the value zone set at the highest of the three, $209.14. Today's price of $468.28 is 124% above it.

The operating figures describe a business performing at a high level; the price asks for that performance to continue and then some. A margin of safety score of 19 is the measure of how little room that leaves for being wrong.
